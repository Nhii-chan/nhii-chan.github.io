The Development of Enterprise Resource Planning

What is ERP?

Software that integrates a company’s core functions into one shared system.

FinanceInventoryProductionHRSales
1960s

Inventory Management

Computers begin tracking stock and reorder points.

1970s

Material Requirements Planning (MRP)

Calculates what to order, and when, from the production schedule.

1980s

Manufacturing Resource Planning (MRP II)

Extends MRP to capacity, scheduling, purchasing and finance.

1990s

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)

One integrated platform links every department; the term ERP is coined.

2000s–Now

Cloud ERP, AI and Real-Time Analytics

Anywhere access, intelligent automation and instant insight.

ERP connects people, processes, and technology to build smarter and more efficient businesses.
